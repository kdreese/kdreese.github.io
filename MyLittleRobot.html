My Little Robot

Game and Development Info

  • Genre:
    Platformer
  • Platform:
    PC (Windows, Linux, Mac, and browser)
  • Project Status:
    Originally created in 96 hours for the 2024 GMTK Game Jam. Released on Itch.io
  • Game Description:
    My Little Robot takes you on the journey of a small robot with big plans! Guide him through a mysterious complex full of puzzles. Upgrade your robot to complete platforming challenges. What is his end goal?
  • Role:
    Gameplay Programmer
  • Tools Used:
    Godot, GitHub
  • Project Duration:
    August 2024
  • Team Size:
    5

Team Responsibilities and Lessons Learned

  • Lead character controller design and implementation
  • Developed character move set with a focus on good game feel
  • Implemented level hazards
  • Set up level tile maps and designed portions of the game levels
  • Managed the communication and asset handoffs between the programming team and the design team
  • Your players won't be masters at your game right from the start
    Though I was very happy with my character implementation, what with its coyote time and buffered jumps and holding jump to jump higher, we got a lot of feedback about the levels being very difficult. The levels were designed with these features in mind by people with combined decades of platformer experience. Because we weren't setting out with a plan to make a difficult platformer, there's a lot we could have improved: having people test our levels to get this feedback before submitting to the jam, level design that doesn't punish the player so much for imperfect play, and better design to teach the player about the advanced mechanics
